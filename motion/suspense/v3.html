You are not mad.
You want that understood.
A madman could never have planned it this carefully.
The old man never wronged you. You even liked him.
It was his eye.
Pale · clouded · like a vulture’s
Your blood ran cold
Seven nights
12:00
Midnight
So slowly it takes an hour.
And you watch him sleep.
On the eighth night, he wakes.
And you hear it.
A low, dull, quick sound.
His heart.
Louder.
And louder.
The heart is silent.
You hide him beneath the floorboards.
So neatly that no eye could find a thing.
The same night
4:00 AM
Three policemen knock.
A neighbour heard a cry.
You smile.
You even set their chairs right over the spot.
They sit. They chat.
And then you hear it again.
Coming from under the floor
They must hear it.
They are only pretending not to.
You cannot bear it
